construction Tool Usage

To begin your assessment, navigate to the "Upload" tab in the top navigation. You'll need to provide video clips following our specific recording guidelines to ensure clinical-grade precision. Our step-by-step wizard will guide you through the requirements for camera placement and environmental lighting.
Our platform is optimized for modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox). For video recording, we recommend a device with at least a 1080p resolution camera. High-speed internet is required for the secure upload of biometric data packets.

analytics Understanding Results

Confidence scores represent the statistical alignment between the uploaded behavioral data and our validated clinical benchmarks. Higher scores indicate a stronger correlation with specific neurodivergent markers identified in our peer-reviewed training sets.

biotech Medical & Science

No, AutismPredictor provides a clinical-grade screening tool, not a definitive medical diagnosis. It is designed to empower parents and healthcare providers with data-driven insights to facilitate further diagnostic discussions with specialized medical professionals.
